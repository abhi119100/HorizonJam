# Multi-Source Harmonic Detection Tournament

## Research Question

Can multi-view harmonic evidence outperform any single transcription or
direct-chord path for short musician performances while preserving useful,
interpretable uncertainty?

## Boundary

This area is `EXPERIMENTAL`. It must not import into the active product, change
the default detector, mutate retrieval data, or feed unverified output to the
tutor. Research adapters execute behind a subprocess/JSON boundary until Gate
6 activation explicitly promotes an implementation through
`detection.run_detection()`.

The frozen comparison baseline is HorizonJam v3. Its archive and verification
instructions are in `docs/releases/V3_BASELINE.md`.

## Product Modes

- Mode A, understand what I played: note, chord, DSP/chroma, segmentation, and
  fusion research on open-ended musician audio.
- Mode B, compare me to a known recording: identification/alignment plus a
  separately licensed expected-harmony source. ShazamKit belongs only here.

## Sequential Gates

1. External feasibility: license, weights, install, security, platform.
2. Baseline tournament: identical audio, labels, metrics, and runtime protocol.
3. Complementarity: determine whether candidates make different errors.
4. Fusion value: compare bounded combinations against the best individual.
5. Product suitability: latency, memory, portability, uncertainty, license.
6. Activation: production adapter, contract tests, full evidence, review.

No gate may be skipped. Synthetic results remain synthetic, and raw model
probabilities are not calibrated HorizonJam confidence.
